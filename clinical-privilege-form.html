Instructions

For existing SLBMC practitioners renewing their clinical privileges
This re-credentialing form is for practitioners already credentialed at SLBMC whose credentialing period is coming to an end. It captures only the information that may have changed since your last cycle; your education, training, and other on-file information do not need to be re-entered. This form must be typed or completed legibly by hand in blue or black ink. If more space is required when answering a question, attach a clearly labelled continuation sheet referencing the section and question number.

VI

Changes Since Last Credentialing Cycle

Report anything that has happened since your previous credentialing

Answer each question in relation to the period since your last credentialing. If Yes, provide details in the box that appears or attach a signed disclosure sheet.

1. Have you been subject to any new disciplinary action by, or do you currently have proceedings pending before, any Medical Board, Professional Association, or regulatory authority?
If Yes, attach a comprehensive written disclosure on a separate signed and dated document.
2. Has any of your medical or professional licences been denied, limited, suspended, revoked or allowed to lapse since your last cycle?
If Yes, attach a comprehensive written disclosure on a separate signed and dated document.
3. Have you been charged with, or convicted of, any criminal offence (excluding minor traffic violations)?
If Yes, attach a comprehensive written disclosure on a separate signed and dated document.
4. Has any new malpractice action been filed, or any previous malpractice action settled or closed, against you?
If Yes, attach a comprehensive written disclosure on a separate signed and dated document.
5. Have any restrictions been placed on your authority to prescribe, dispense, or handle controlled substances?
If Yes, attach a comprehensive written disclosure on a separate signed and dated document.
6. Have you been the subject of any disciplinary measures, investigations, or restrictions at any hospital or health institution (including SLBMC)?
If Yes, attach a comprehensive written disclosure on a separate signed and dated document.
7. Have you resigned, declined to renew, or had reduced, any clinical privileges or medical staff membership at any institution during this period?
If Yes, attach a comprehensive written disclosure on a separate signed and dated document.
